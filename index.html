The MITTI story

MITTI (मिट्टी) means “earth” — the material from which all art is born. We are an Indian art brand dedicated to bringing authentic, handcrafted pieces from the heart of India into your home.

Every artwork in our collection is created by skilled artisans and artists who work with time-honoured techniques—from the mud-and-mirror craft of Kutch, Gujarat, to contemporary landscape and abstract paintings that capture the soul of India.
