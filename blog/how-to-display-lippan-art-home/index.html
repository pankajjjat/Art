How to Display Lippan Art in Your Home: A Complete Guide

So you've fallen in love with a piece of lippan art. Now comes the next question: how do you display it to best effect? Here's a complete guide — from lighting to placement — to make your mirror-work piece shine.

Lighting Is Everything

Lippan art was designed to interact with light. The mirrors embedded in the clay catch and scatter ambient light, creating a dynamic visual experience that changes throughout the day.

  • Natural light: Place the piece where it receives indirect sunlight at different times of day. Morning and golden-hour light bring out warm tones in terracotta and white clay.
  • Accent lighting: A picture light or track light positioned at a 30-degree angle creates dramatic shadow patterns from the raised clay.
  • Backlighting (advanced): For larger pieces, LED backlighting behind the panel creates a stunning halo effect — the mirrors glow against the dark.

Wall Colour Matters

The background wall colour dramatically affects how lippan art reads:

  • Dark walls (deep blue, charcoal, forest green): Make the mirrors pop dramatically. The contrast is striking and the piece becomes a focal point.
  • Light walls (cream, warm grey, white): Emphasise the texture and raised patterns. More subtle, elegant, and minimalist.
  • Earthy walls (terracotta, ochre, sage): Create a harmonious monochromatic look where the piece blends with its environment.

Placement Guidelines

  • Eye level: The centre of the piece should be at 145–155 cm from the floor (typical art gallery height).
  • Space around it: Give lippan art breathing room — at least 15 cm clearance on each side.
  • Avoid direct sunlight: Prolonged direct UV exposure can fade natural clay pigments over time.
  • Avoid high-humidity areas: While sealed, lippan pieces are best kept away from direct steam sources.

Framing or Unframed?

Most modern lippan pieces come mounted on a wooden or MDF board and can be hung directly. A floating frame adds a contemporary touch, while a shadow box frame creates additional depth. At MITTI, we deliver pieces ready to hang — no framing needed unless you want a specific look.

Pairing with Other Art

Lippan art pairs beautifully with brass accents, wooden decor, and indoor plants. Avoid pairing it with overly busy patterned wallpapers — the mirror work needs a calm background to shine.
