Modern Lippan Wall Decor: 7 Stunning Ideas for Your Home

Thinking of adding something unique to your walls? Forget generic framed prints — modern lippan art brings texture, light, and centuries of Indian craftsmanship into your home. Here are 7 ways to incorporate it into your decor.

1. The Statement Focal Point

Choose a large lippan piece (36" x 48" or larger) and let it dominate a feature wall — above a console table in the living room, behind the dining table, or at the end of a hallway. The mirror work will catch light from multiple angles, making it the room's natural centrepiece.

2. Minimalist White & Gold

For modern and Scandinavian-style interiors, white lippan art with gold-leaf accents provides texture without visual clutter. The raised patterns create subtle shadows — a perfect balance between tradition and minimalism.

3. Terracotta & Teal — A Colour Story

Pair warm terracotta clay lippan pieces with teal or navy walls for a rich, earthy contrast. This combination evokes Indian desert landscapes and brings a cosy, grounded energy to any room.

4. Create a Lippan Gallery Wall

Mix smaller lippan pieces (12"–18") with other textured wall elements — macramé, brass mirrors, or wooden masks. The variety of materials creates an eclectic, collected-over-time look.

5. Bedroom Serenity

Above the bed, a circular or mandala lippan piece can serve as an alternative to a traditional headboard. The mirrors provide soft reflected light — calming and meditative.

6. Bathroom Spa Vibes

Yes — lippan art in the bathroom! The natural clay and mirror texture creates an instant spa atmosphere. Opt for smaller, sealed pieces away from direct water contact.

7. Custom Name or Mantra Art

Personalised lippan pieces with names, family mottos, or Sanskrit mantras make meaningful gifts and powerful personal talismans. Each piece is entirely unique.

At MITTI, every lippan piece is handcrafted. No two are identical — because your home deserves art that's as individual as you are.
